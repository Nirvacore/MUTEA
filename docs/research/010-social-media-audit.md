# Report 010 — Thai Social Media Landscape Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-05
**Status:** Complete
**Phase:** 1 (Market Intelligence)

## Executive Summary

Thailand's social media landscape presents a unique three-platform opportunity for Mu Tea: Instagram for brand aesthetic and discovery, TikTok for rapid growth and education, and LINE for conversion and retention. TikTok dominates the Thai influencer market (66% of campaigns), with 400,000+ creators. Instagram remains essential for premium brand positioning. LINE is the non-negotiable conversion channel with 51M Thai users and 80-90% message read rates. No competitor currently occupies the "mindful tea education" content niche on any Thai platform.

## Instagram Audit

### Thai Matcha Instagram Landscape

**Dominant content types:**
1. Café visit photos (location tags, latte art)
2. "Aesthetic matcha" flat lays
3. Recipe videos (matcha lattes, desserts)
4. Brand promotions (sweetened matcha products)

**What's missing:**
- Cultivar-specific content (Okumidori vs Samidori etc.)
- Processing education (shade-growing, stone-milling)
- Ritual preparation (beyond "how to make a latte")
- Terroir stories (Uji vs Kagoshima vs Shizuoka)
- Mindfulness-positioned tea content

### Hashtag Analysis

| Category | Hashtags | Est. Volume |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Primary matcha** | #matcha #greentea #matchalatte | 10M+ posts each |
| **Enthusiast** | #matchalover #matchagreentea #ceremonialmatcha | 100K-1M posts |
| **Lifestyle** | #MindfulLiving #WellnessJourney #SelfCareSunday | 1M-5M posts |
| **Niche** | #japanesetea #teaceremony #matchawhisk #ujimatcha | 10K-100K posts |
| **Thai** | #มัทฉะ #ชาเขียว #ชาญี่ปุ่น #ชามัทฉะ | 50K-500K posts |

**Optimal strategy:** 3-5 hashtags per post. Mix 1 high-volume (#matcha) + 2 niche (#ceremonialmatcha #japanesetea) + 1-2 Thai (#มัทฉะ #ชาเขียว).

### Instagram Algorithm 2026

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Reels reach | Highest organic reach of any format |
| Carousel saves | Highest save rate — signals value to algorithm |
| Stories | Daily touchpoint, not discovery — relationship maintenance |
| Posting frequency | 5-7 posts/week optimal for algorithm visibility |
| Best content signals | Saves > Shares > Comments > Likes |

**Implication for Mu Tea:** Optimize for saves (educational carousels) and shares (ritual Reels), not likes.

## TikTok Audit

### Thai TikTok Landscape

### TikTok Matcha Content Analysis

**Trending content formats:**
1. "Things I learned about [X]" — education hooks
2. ASMR preparation — sound-first matcha content
3. "I switched from coffee to matcha for 30 days" — transformation stories
4. Blind taste tests — interactive engagement
5. "Did you know?" — quick facts with visual proof
6. Recipe/preparation tutorials — how-to format

**Hashtag strategy for TikTok:**

| Type | Examples | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Trending (1-2) | #matcha #greentea | Broad discovery |
| Niche (2-3) | #matchalover #japanesetea #teaceremony | Targeted audience |
| Branded (1) | #MuTea #VoidToVoid | Brand tracking |
| Thai (1-2) | #มัทฉะ #ชาเขียวญี่ปุ่น | Local discovery |

**Rule:** 3-5 total hashtags per video. More than 5 reduces reach.

### TikTok Opportunity for Mu Tea

**Why TikTok matters more than Instagram for growth:**
- Algorithm serves content to non-followers (discovery engine)
- Education content performs well (not just entertainment)
- 400K+ Thai creators = potential collaboration pool
- +114% hashtag traffic growth = expanding organic reach
- Matcha content is trending but education is underserved

**Content angles unique to Mu Tea:**
- "Why this matcha costs 10x more than that one" (price education)
- "The science of shade-growing" (L-Theanine story without health claims)
- "800 years of matcha in 60 seconds" (history hooks)
- "Reading your matcha tin like a sommelier" (grade/cultivar education)
- "The quietest 90 seconds of your day" (ritual ASMR)

## LINE Official Account Audit

### Why LINE is Non-Negotiable for Thai DTC

| Metric | LINE | Email | Instagram DM |
|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| Thai user base | 51M (85.7%) | Variable | 23M |
| Read rate | 80-90% | 10-15% | ~50% |
| Commerce integration | LINE MyShop | Requires website | Limited |
| Payment integration | Native | External | None |
| Customer relationship | Conversational | Transactional | Informal |

### LINE MyShop Capabilities

- List products with photos, descriptions, pricing
- Accept orders and process payments
- No separate website or e-commerce platform needed
- Integrates with LINE chat for customer support
- Transaction history and customer data

**Mu Tea implication:** LINE MyShop can serve as the Phase 1 commerce platform, eliminating the need for a Shopify/website build before first sales.

### LINE Content Strategy for Mu Tea

**Weekly broadcast (1/week max to prevent blocks):**

| Week | Content | CTA |
|------|---------|-----|
| Week 1 | Cultivar spotlight (education) | "Which would you like to try?" |
| Week 2 | Brewing tip or seasonal recommendation | Link to product |
| Week 3 | Customer story or behind-the-scenes | "Tell us your tea moment" |
| Week 4 | New product or limited offer | Direct purchase link |

**Segmentation strategy:**
- Tag friends by collection interest (Earth / Space / Sun)
- Tag by source (Instagram, packaging QR, event)
- Tag by purchase history (prospect / first-purchase / repeat)
- Send targeted content based on tags, not mass blasts

### LINE Friend Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Intent Level | Cost | Priority |

| Checkout/order confirmation | Very high | Free | Phase 1 |
| Instagram bio link | High | Free | Phase 1 |

## Influencer Landscape

### Recommended Influencer Tiers for Mu Tea

| Tier | Followers | Why | Cost (est.) |
|------|-----------|-----|-------------|
| **Nano** (Phase 1) | 1K-10K | Authentic, high engagement, affordable | ฿1,000-5,000/post or product exchange |
| **Micro** (Phase 2) | 10K-50K | Targeted reach, trusted recommendations | ฿5,000-20,000/post |
| **Mid** (Phase 3+) | 50K-200K | Significant reach with credibility | ฿20,000-80,000/post |

**Influencer selection criteria for Mu Tea:**
1. Genuine interest in tea, wellness, or mindfulness (not just "lifestyle")
2. Aesthetic alignment (calm, minimal, intentional — not flashy)
3. Thai + English bilingual content (for future regional expansion)
4. Not promoting competing matcha brands
5. Engagement rate >3% (quality over quantity)

**Phase 1 target:** 3-5 nano-influencers who genuinely love tea. Product exchange, not paid promotion. Authentic relationship > reach.
