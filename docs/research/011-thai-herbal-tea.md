# Report 011 — Thai Herbal Tea: The "Start Now" Path

**Date:** 2026-07-12
**Status:** Complete
**Phase:** 0-1 (Pre-launch — can begin immediately)

## Why This Report Matters

The Japanese tea line (Reports 001-010) is blocked by the **สบ.1 import license** (15-30 days) plus per-SKU อย. import notification, customs, and JTEPA verification. That is the right long-term spine — but it means Mu Tea cannot legally sell Japanese matcha for weeks to months.

**Thai herbal tea (ชาสมุนไพรไทย) removes the import blocker entirely.** The herbs are grown and processed in Thailand, so there is no import license, no customs, no HS-code duty. Mu Tea can source domestically, register a domestic food product, and be selling *while* the Japanese line clears regulatory. This is the fastest path to a first real sale.

Strategically it is also **on-brand, not a detour**: Thai herbal infusions are caffeine-free, evening-friendly, and deeply rooted in a local tradition of slowing down — a natural fit for the **Mu Earth** (grounding/evening/calm) collection and for a Thai audience that already knows and trusts these herbs.

### Signature blend opportunity

The most commercially proven Thai blend is **Lemongrass + Pandan + Butterfly Pea** — caffeine-free, naturally blue, "relax and unwind" positioning. Already sold by Chaidim, Suwirun, and others internationally. This is a validated recipe Mu Tea can start from and refine, not invent from scratch.

**Mu Tea "Blue Hour" concept (Mu Earth):** Butterfly pea + pandan + a touch of lemongrass. The color shift when you add lemon becomes the ritual moment — a visible transformation that mirrors "Void to Void." Caffeine-free = the true evening tea the Japanese line cannot offer (even hojicha has some caffeine).

## Health-Claims Rule (Same as Japanese Line)

Thailand's อย. rules prohibit health/therapeutic claims on ordinary food products. **Everything from Report 005 applies here:**

- ❌ Cannot say: "reduces stress," "aids digestion," "detoxifies," "boosts immunity"
- ✅ Can say: traditional-use *cultural* framing, sensory experience, "caffeine-free," origin story
- ✅ Frame around **feeling and ritual**, not medical benefit

> "Thai grandmothers have steeped lemongrass and pandan for generations — not as medicine, but as the smell of coming home."

Note: if Mu Tea ever wants explicit herbal *health* claims, that requires registering as a **ผลิตภัณฑ์สมุนไพร (herbal product)** — a heavier, separate track from ordinary food registration. **Recommendation: stay in the ordinary-food lane** and use experience narrative. Do not over-engineer.

## Regulatory Path (Domestic Food — Much Simpler Than Import)

Because the product is made in Thailand, the import chain (สบ.1 → อย. import → customs → JTEPA) does **not** apply. Instead:

| Step | What | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| 1. Choose production model | OEM factory (recommended) vs own production | OEM factory usually already holds the production-site license + GMP/HACCP |
| 2. Product registration | อย. food registration via **e-Submission** (online) | Formula 100%, production method, site photos, labeling |
| 3. Where to file | One Stop Service Center (OSSC) or สำนักงานสาธารณสุขจังหวัด (provincial) | e-Submission handles most of it online |
| 4. Thai labeling | Ingredients, net weight, producer, อย. number, best-before | Same labeling discipline as Report 004 |
| 5. Sell | LINE MyShop / IG / market | No customs, no import duty |

**Key advantage:** If you use an OEM factory that already has GMP/HACCP and its อย. production license, you inherit their site approval — you only register the *product formula*, which is dramatically faster and cheaper than building your own licensed facility.

## Recommended Sequencing

1. **Now:** Contact 2-3 domestic OEM factories, request MOQ/price/samples
2. **Now (parallel):** Register business entity if not done (needed for อย. + wholesale anyway)
3. **Week 2-4:** Taste samples, choose 1-2 signature blends (start with lemongrass-pandan-butterfly pea)
4. **Week 4-8:** Finalize formula, OEM handles อย. food registration
5. **Week 6-10:** Packaging design (shared brand identity with future Japanese line)
6. **Week 10-12:** First stock ready → sell via LINE MyShop + IG
7. **Parallel throughout:** Japanese line สบ.1 → อย. import runs in the background; launches later as the premium tier

**Net effect:** Mu Tea makes its first real sale in ~10-12 weeks with a caffeine-free, on-brand, domestically-sourced product — instead of waiting months for the import chain, while the Japanese line becomes the premium follow-on rather than a launch blocker.
