# Report 009 — Content Marketing Strategy

**Date:** 2026-07-05
**Status:** Complete
**Phase:** 1-2 (Brand Building)

## Executive Summary

Mu Tea's content strategy must center on farm-to-cup storytelling, tea education in Thai language, and ritual documentation — not product promotion. Research confirms UGC is 3.5x more trusted than branded content, and 78% of shoppers say detailed product photography influences purchase decisions. The recommended content mix: 40% education, 30% engagement, 20% product, 10% community. LINE Official Account is the primary conversion channel for Thai DTC (51M users, 80-90% read rates vs email's 10-15%).

## Content Pillars

### The 5 Pillars for Mu Tea

| Pillar | Share | Content Type | Goal |
|--------|-------|-------------|------|
| **Tea Knowledge** | 40% | Cultivar profiles, terroir stories, processing education, L-Theanine science (experience-framed) | Authority + saves |
| **Ritual & Practice** | 30% | Preparation videos, morning routines, whisking technique, seasonal tea moments | Engagement + shares |
| **Product & Collection** | 15% | Product photography, unboxing, tasting notes, collection stories | Conversion |
| **Community** | 10% | Customer stories, UGC reshares, Q&A, tea circle moments | Trust + loyalty |
| **Behind the Scenes** | 5% | Farm visits, sourcing stories, founder reflections, packaging process | Authenticity |

### Why This Balance

- **40% education** builds the content moat — no one else creates Thai-language cultivar education
- **30% ritual** aligns with "Void to Void" philosophy — showing the practice, not selling the product
- **15% product** is enough for conversion without becoming a sales channel
- **10% community** leverages the UGC trust multiplier (3.5x more trusted than branded posts)
- **5% behind the scenes** humanizes without over-sharing

## Platform Strategy

### Instagram (Primary Discovery)

| Format | Purpose | Frequency | Mu Tea Application |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| Reels | Discovery + reach | 3-4/week | Whisking rituals, farm stories, "matcha vs coffee" education |
| Carousels | Education + saves | 2-3/week | Cultivar comparisons, brewing guides, collection explainers |
| Stories | Daily relationship | Daily | Morning tea moment, polls, behind-the-scenes |
| Static posts | Brand aesthetic | 1-2/week | Product photography, quotes, mood shots |

**Optimal posting:** 5-7 posts per week for algorithm performance.

**Content that works for wellness/mindfulness brands:**
- Time-lapse preparation videos (matcha whisking from powder to froth)
- Side-by-side cultivar comparisons (color, foam, taste descriptions)
- "A moment with..." series — short, quiet, unscripted tea moments
- Seasonal content tied to Japanese tea calendar (shincha, autumn hojicha)

### LINE Official Account (Primary Conversion)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Thai LINE users | 51 million (85.7% of internet users) |
| Message read rate | 80-90% |
| Email read rate (comparison) | 10-15% |
| Commerce | LINE MyShop — list products, orders, payments without separate website |

**LINE is Mu Tea's most important channel for Thailand.** Not Instagram, not TikTok — LINE is where conversion happens.

**Friend Growth Strategy:**
- Map different QR codes to each touchpoint (IG bio, packaging, events, checkout)
- Focus on high-intent sources: product pages, checkout, parcel inserts
- Budget separately for high-intent capture vs broad acquisition
- Track cost-per-friend AND cost-per-buyer (not just follower count)

**Onboarding Sequence (72 hours):**
1. **Immediate:** Welcome message + deliver promised value (e.g., brewing guide PDF)
2. **Within 24 hours:** Request preferences via taps (which collection interests them?)
3. **Day 3:** One relevant offer based on stated preferences

**Benchmarks to hit:**
- Broadcast open rate: >60%
- Block rate: <5%
- Track: first-purchase rate, repeat purchase within 60-90 days, revenue per 1,000 friends

**SME approach (Mu Tea Phase 1):**
- Master high-intent friend capture first
- Simple automations (welcome sequence, order confirmation)
- Segment by collection interest (Mu Earth / Mu Space / Mu Sun)

## Storytelling Framework

### The Mu Tea Narrative Layers

**Layer 1 — The Tea** (Product truth)
> "This is Samidori cultivar, shade-grown for 20 days in Uji, Kyoto. Stone-milled by a family that has been making matcha for three generations."

**Layer 2 — The Practice** (Ritual truth)
> "Heat the water to 80°C. Sift 2 grams. Whisk in a W-motion until the surface is a jade foam. These 90 seconds are yours."

**Layer 3 — The Return** (Philosophical truth)
> "This is not productivity tea. This is not wellness tea. This is tea that helps you remember you're already whole."

### What NOT to do

- **No health claims** — อย. rules prohibit it; experience narrative is stronger anyway
- **No comparison attacks** — "better than coffee" is positioning; "coffee is bad" is attacking
- **No performative mindfulness** — if it looks like a meditation ad, it's wrong
- **No influencer voice** — Mu Tea speaks quietly; let the tea speak
- **No algorithm chasing** — consistent quality > viral moments
