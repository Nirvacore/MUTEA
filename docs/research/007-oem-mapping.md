# Report 007 — OEM & Private Label Mapping

**Date:** 2026-07-05
**Status:** Complete
**Phase:** 3 (Private Label — future reference)

## Executive Summary

Comprehensive mapping of Japan-based OEM and private label matcha manufacturers for Mu Tea's Phase 3 (private label development). Key finding: private label is accessible at 50-100kg MOQ with 6-12 week turnaround; full OEM starts at 500kg+ with 16-32 weeks. Aiya has paused private label services — AOI Matcha, MatchaKyoto, Keicha, and First Agri are active alternatives. Yunomi also offers private label services, which aligns with Mu Tea's existing wholesale relationship.

## Private Label vs OEM: Decision Framework

| Dimension | Private Label | Custom Blending | Full OEM |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| Definition | Existing product + your label | Supplier product with blend adjustments | Custom formulation to your spec |
| MOQ | 50-100 kg | 250 kg | 500+ kg |
| Timeline | 6-12 weeks | 10-16 weeks | 16-32 weeks |
| Cost premium | Base price | 5-10% over base | 15-25% over base |
| Development cost | None | $1,000-5,000 | $3,000-20,000+ |
| Regulatory dossier | Supplier provides | Supplier provides | $5,000-15,000 additional |
| IP ownership | Supplier's formulation | Shared | Your formulation (with contract) |
| Control | Low (label only) | Medium (blend choice) | High (full spec) |

**Mu Tea recommendation:** Start with **Private Label** in Phase 3 (lowest risk, fastest to market), evolve to **Custom Blending** once volumes justify 250kg MOQ, and consider **Full OEM** only in Phase 4-5 when brand identity demands unique formulations.

## Packaging Format Options

| Format | Best for | MOQ (typical) | Unit cost |
|--------|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Tin (30g) | Premium gift, ceremonial matcha | 140+ units | $2-4/tin |
| Tin (80g) | Standard retail | 80+ units | $3-5/tin |
| Resealable pouch (100g) | Value/everyday | 100+ units | $1-2/pouch |
| Stick packs (2g × 10) | Subscription box, travel, trial | 200+ boxes | $2-3/box |
| Bulk bag (200-500g) | Café supply, repeat customers | 50+ units | $3-6/bag |

**Mu Tea Phase 3 format strategy:**
- Start with **stick packs** for subscription boxes (most versatile, shareable)
- Add **resealable pouches** for repeat customers
- **Tins** for premium/gift positioning only

## Key Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| MOQ too high for current volume | Cash tied up in inventory | Start with Yunomi private label (lowest MOQ) |
| Quality inconsistency between batches | Brand damage | Request CoA per batch; taste-test before release |
| IP not protected in contract | Supplier sells "your" blend to others | Full OEM contract with exclusivity clause |
| Aiya reopens with better terms | Missed opportunity | Monitor quarterly |
| Exchange rate JPY/THB volatility | Cost unpredictability | Lock rates for large orders; maintain THB reserve |
